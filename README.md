# Groww: Increasing 12-Month SIP Retention for First-Time Investors
> Graduation Project • Product Management (Growth & Retention)

## Executive Summary
This project tackles the systemic drop-off of first-time mutual fund SIP investors on Groww, where 62% of users lapse or pause before Month 12. Using an AI-powered discovery engine analyzing 1,240+ public reviews and Reddit threads, we identified that **68% of churn clusters in Months 3–5 ("The Disillusionment Valley")**, driven by rigid auto-debit bank bounce penalties (₹250–₹500) and the "Expense Perception Trap" (underwhelming early returns).

Our solution, the **Groww Smart SIP Horizon & Cashflow Copilot**, introduces proactive cashflow buffers (Smart Date Flex & Penalty-Free Skip) and an eval-gated, SEBI-compliant educational copilot.
