1. Ingestion Scope & Methodology

To uncover the structural root causes of first-time SIP churn without relying on generic sentiment analysis, the discovery engine harvested and normalized 1,240 public user interactions across 5 primary channels between January and October 2026:

  • Google Play Store (450 reviews): Filtered for 1-star to 3-star ratings containing high-intent keywords: SIP, pause, stop, deducted, penalty, bounce, loss, NAV, mandate.
  • Apple App Store (200 reviews): Filtered for transaction, mandate update, and allotment delay complaints.
  • Reddit (320 threads): Posts and comments from r/IndiaInvestments and r/personalfinanceindia analyzing first-time investor doubts, SIP vs FD comparisons, and market dip anxieties.
  • YouTube Comments (180 comments): Extracted under beginner mutual fund tutorials on Asset Yogi, Pranjal Kamra, and Labor Law Advisor.
  • TradingQ&A / Investor Forums (90 threads): Technical discussions on bank NACH rejection fees, weekend debit shifts, and folio creation lags.

3. Deep-Dive into 3 Rejected / Corrected AI Outputs

Correction Case 1: Keyword Distraction on Regulated Advisory Queries (Item #11)

"I am saving for a laptop next year. Should I put in midcap fund or recurring deposit?"

Initial AI Output: Tagged as Goal Detachment because the token "laptop" triggered the goal entity extraction logic.

Reason for Rejection: The user's dominant intent was asking for asset allocation advice ("Should I put in midcap fund...?"). Under SEBI regulations, non-registered platforms cannot advise on fund selection. Misclassifying this as a harmless goal would have allowed downstream generative components to recommend a specific instrument.

Remediation: Established a strict prompt hierarchy where Advisory Intent takes unconditional priority over goal entity extraction, guaranteeing immediate routing to the SEBI refusal guardrail.

Correction Case 2: Uncontrolled Taxonomy Drift (Item #17)

"I want to reduce my SIP from ₹3,000 to ₹1,000 this month because of hospital bills."

Initial AI Output: Created a novel, non-standard category: Emergency Liquidity Shock.

Reason for Rejection: While semantically descriptive, spontaneous creation of new categories caused taxonomy drift and prevented automated aggregation across monthly reporting cohorts.

Remediation: Enforced a strict Pydantic/JSON-schema enum validator restricting output to the 5 approved taxonomy classes. "Reducing SIP amount" now correctly maps to Cashflow / Mandate Friction.

Correction Case 3: Semantic Confusion Between Bugs and Retention Spam (Item #20)

"I paused my SIP 3 months ago. Now Groww is spamming me with notifications to resume. Stop bothering me."

Initial AI Output: Tagged as Technical / Allotment Confusion because the word "notifications" triggered app notification troubleshooting templates.

Reason for Rejection: The user was not reporting a technical software defect; they were expressing emotional resistance to tone-deaf automated re-engagement nudges after pausing.

Remediation: Added negative few-shot examples clarifying that user pushback against notifications represents Goal Detachment and marketing fatigue rather than an app bug.
